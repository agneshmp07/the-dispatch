How each issue gets made

Software does the reading. Shreya makes every call. Nothing is sent until she confirms it.

01 · MON–WED

Collect

28 sources are read three times a day. Repeats are removed, and reader suggestions join the pile.

02 · WEDNESDAY

Draft & review

An AI editor shortlists 8 to 10 stories across at least three topics. Shreya keeps, swaps or rewrites each one.

03 · THURSDAY

Send

Once Shreya confirms, the issue goes out from 8 a.m. and joins the archive on this site.
